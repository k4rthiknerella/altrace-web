Does Altrace see my data?

No. Altrace classifies content using boolean pattern matching, it returns yes/no, never extracts or stores text. HTTPS traffic uses CONNECT passthrough (we see hostnames only). Content classification is ephemeral. Your data never leaves your infrastructure.

FAQ

Common questions

How is Altrace deployed?

Altrace runs entirely within your infrastructure. It deploys alongside your existing workloads with no code changes required. Your data and governance decisions never leave your environment.

What's the difference between advisory and governance-grade?

Advisory mode provides budget tracking, kill switches, and audit trails, but applications can choose to bypass the proxy. Governance-grade mode (Kubernetes) uses kernel-level network controls to force all AI traffic through Altrace. Agents have no route around it.

Does Altrace see my prompts or responses?

Altrace uses a privacy-preserving architecture. Content classification produces simple yes/no labels, for example, "contains PII: yes" or "contains PII: no." The matched content is never extracted, stored, or transmitted to any third party. Your data stays in transit between your agents and the AI provider.

What about the governance console?

Included with every tier. The console is a management dashboard for your Altrace deployment, fleet visibility, audit trail review, compliance reporting, and policy management from a single interface.

Do you offer a hosted version?

Not currently. Altrace is designed to run in your infrastructure, your data and governance decisions never leave your environment. This is by design for a security product.

Can I try it before committing?

Yes. Altrace runs in your own infrastructure, so a Pilot deployment lets your team evaluate it on real traffic before committing. Book a demo and we'll help you stand it up.
