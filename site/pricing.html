FAQ

Common questions

How is Altrace deployed?

Altrace runs in your infrastructure. In Kubernetes, it deploys as a sidecar proxy via Helm chart with an init container for network enforcement. In Docker, it runs as a standalone container. Your data and governance decisions never leave your environment.

What is the difference between advisory and governance-grade?

Advisory mode (Docker) provides budget tracking, kill switches, and audit trails, but applications can bypass the proxy by not routing through it. Governance-grade (Kubernetes with iptables or Cilium) uses kernel-level network rules to force all LLM traffic through Altrace — agents have no route around it.

Does Altrace see my prompts or responses?

Altrace uses content-blind architecture. Classification uses RE2 regex patterns that produce boolean labels only (e.g., "contains PII: true/false"). The matched content is never extracted, stored, or transmitted. Your data stays in transit between your agents and the LLM provider.

What about the governance console?

Included with Team and Enterprise tiers. The console is a web application that connects to your Altrace hub. It ships with the Helm chart and provides fleet management, audit trail, compliance reporting, and policy management.

Do you offer a hosted version?

Not currently. Altrace is designed to run in your infrastructure — your data and governance decisions never leave your environment. This is by design for a security product.

Can I try it before committing?

Yes. The Community tier is free and runs in Docker Compose. You can have a working governance proxy scanning real LLM traffic in under 2 minutes. No credit card, no sales call.
