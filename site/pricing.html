Does Altrace see my data?

No. Altrace checks your data using simple yes/no security checks—like flagging whether sensitive information is present—without ever reading, copying, or storing your actual text. All secure traffic passes straight through without exposing your content, meaning your information is never saved and never leaves your private environment.
