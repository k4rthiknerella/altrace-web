FAQ

Common questions

How is Altrace deployed?

Altrace runs in your Kubernetes cluster as a sidecar proxy. Your data and governance decisions never leave your infrastructure. We assist with deployment, architecture review, and ongoing support.

What about the governance console?

Included. The console is a web application that connects to your Altrace hub. It ships with the Helm chart and provides fleet management, audit trail, compliance reporting, and policy management.

Do you offer a hosted version?

Not currently. Altrace is designed to run in your infrastructure — your data and governance decisions never leave your environment. This is by design for a security product.
