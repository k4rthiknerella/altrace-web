The Structural Problem

Prompt guardrails are suggestions.

A jailbroken agent ignores them. A confused agent forgets them. A creative agent works around them. You need controls that work whether the agent cooperates or not.

The Regulatory Moment

EU AI Act deadline: August 2, 2026.

Regulators now require demonstrable human oversight, documented decision records, and tamper-evident audit trails. If your AI agents cannot prove compliance, your organization is exposed.

The August 2 deadline covers Article 50 transparency obligations and penalty provisions only. High-risk obligations under Annex III have been delayed to December 2027 per the EU Digital Omnibus package agreed by the Council on March 13, 2026.

What enforcement looks like

Four things monitoring cannot do.

Block before the damage

Monitoring detects a cost overrun after $50K is spent. Altrace blocks the request that would exceed the $500 budget — before it reaches the provider.

Require evidence before action

Monitoring flags that an agent called a restricted tool. Altrace blocks the tool call unless the agent has completed prerequisite checks within the last 5 minutes.

Quarantine in real time

Monitoring discovers an unknown agent after it has been active for days. Altrace detects anomalous behavioral patterns and auto-escalates to quarantine — before the agent does damage.

Prevent the connection

Monitoring logs that data was sent to an unauthorized endpoint. Altrace's kernel-level rules prevent the connection from being established.
