Our Belief

AI agents will run critical business processes.

The question isn't whether to deploy them — it's whether you can govern them when something goes wrong. When an agent exceeds its budget. When a delegation chain escalates authority. When a compliance audit asks what happened at 2:23 PM on a Tuesday.

We believe the governance layer for autonomous agents must be infrastructure, not instructions. It must be enforced at a layer agents cannot control. And it must produce records that auditors can verify. That's what we're building.
