The governance layer
for AI agents.

Altrace sits between your agents and the models they call. A multistage governance engine controls what they can access, how much they can spend, and what they are allowed to do — whether they cooperate or not.

What Altrace controls

Nine enforcement domains. Every decision, before it reaches the model.

01

Kill switches

Stop any agent instantly — by team, by agent, or globally. One click to shut down all AI activity.

02

Spend limits

Hard budget ceilings per team and per agent. Every request is checked before it reaches the model.

03

Content governance

Bidirectional scanning — detect sensitive data and policy violations in both inbound requests and outbound responses, automatically.

04

Tool authorization

Control which tools each agent can use. Block unregistered or unauthorized tools before they execute.

05

Evidence grounding

Require agents to look up data before taking action. No refund without an order lookup first.

06

Anomaly detection

Discover unknown agents on your network. Automatically escalate when behavior deviates from baseline.

07

Graduated response

Five escalation levels — from monitoring to full quarantine. Behavioral fingerprinting detects drift. De-escalation is deliberately slower than escalation.

08

Streaming governance

Real-time stream scanning with cross fragment reassembly. Sensitive data detected midstream triggers immediate cancellation.

09

Agent identity

Enterprise identity provisioning, single sign-on, zero-trust service identity, and role-based access control.

How it's different

Governance that agents cannot circumvent.

Most tools rely on the application to enforce rules — which means a misconfigured or compromised agent can ignore them. Altrace enforces at the infrastructure layer, below the application.

Infrastructure-level enforcement

All AI traffic passes through Altrace before it reaches any model. No code changes required. Governance is mandatory, not optional.

Your data never leaves

Runs inside your infrastructure. No content is extracted, stored, or transmitted.

Tamper-evident audit trail

Every governance decision is recorded in an immutable chain. Prove exactly what happened, when, and why — to any auditor.
