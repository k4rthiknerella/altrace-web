Built for security teams

The controls your security org already asks for.

The section your security questionnaire is really about, enforced on the control plane.

Mutual TLS

Client certs verified against your CA; cert identity captured in the audit trail. Fail-closed in production.

Team-scoped RBAC

Operator roles (admin / operator / viewer / agent). A team-scoped token can’t reach another team’s kill switches, budgets, or credentials.

Token revocation & lockout

Revoke a compromised operator token instantly, with a tamper-evident record. Per-IP / per-token brute-force lockout.

IP / CIDR allowlisting

Restrict the control API to known networks, with explicit forwarded-header trust control. Defense in depth.

Durable SIEM delivery

Every state-mutating control action is fsync-persisted before acknowledgment, then reliably forwarded to your SIEM.

OpenAPI 3.1 contract

A typed, reviewable control-API specification, 23 endpoints your team can audit and integrate against.
